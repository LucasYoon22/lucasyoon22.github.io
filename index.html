About Me

My interests lie at the intersection of rigorous mathematical theory, statistical inference, and computational methods. I enjoy building data-driven systems that combine theoretical understanding with practical implementation.

Research

My research interests lie at the intersection of Machine Learning, Statistical Inference, and Mathematical Analysis, with a particular focus on developing mathematically grounded and computationally efficient learning algorithms. My work explores high-dimensional regression, foundation models, in-context learning, statistical optimization, and predictive modeling. I am especially interested in understanding how mathematical structures and statistical principles can improve model generalization, interpretability, and computational efficiency. Through my research in high-dimensional linear regression and applied machine learning, I aim to bridge theoretical mathematics with modern artificial intelligence and real-world applications.

I am especially interested in foundation models, in-context learning, sparse regression, statistical optimization, and understanding how machine learning models generalize across different data distributions.

Machine Learning Research

KALBI: Krylov Adaptive Linear Basis In-context Learning for High-Dimensional Linear Regression Foundation Model

Yonsei University · Machine Learning Research

Research on foundation models for high-dimensional linear regression, investigating in-context learning approaches for sparse and dense regression tasks.

Developed components of an end-to-end pretraining pipeline using PyTorch, including synthetic data generation for different regression settings. The research explores how models can learn adaptive regression behavior from data while reducing reliance on repeated hyperparameter selection.
