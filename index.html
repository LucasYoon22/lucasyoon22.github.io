Undergraduate Research · Yonsei University

KALBI — Krylov Adaptive Linear Basis In-context Learning for High-Dimensional Linear Regression

Machine Learning Lab · Jun–Aug 2026 · Advisor: Prof. Minseok Shin

Co-authored research on a Transformer-free foundation model for high-dimensional sparse regression (up to 200,000 features) with CPU inference. Work focused on synthetic pretraining pipelines, robust regression benchmarks, and understanding model behavior across sparse and dense tasks.

  • Profiled synthetic data generation and traced 43 of 44 seconds in one structural causal model generator to feature screening; identified fallback noise in 71% of its features.
  • Introduced per-generator configuration overrides, reducing data generation time from 228 to 130 seconds (1.75× faster), with identical outputs verified across 20 tasks.
  • Added a parallel Bayesian neural network branch to broaden the synthetic pretraining mixture and identified a potential further 22× speedup in tree-based generators.
  • Benchmarked against cross-validated LASSO, SCAD, and MCP on 41 real datasets; investigated a correlation-filter bottleneck that reduced effective feature dimensionality.
