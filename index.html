Research

My research interests lie at the intersection of Machine Learning, Statistical Inference, and Mathematical Analysis. As a Statistics and Mathematics student at the University of Toronto, I am particularly interested in developing mathematically grounded, interpretable, and robust machine learning models. My work spans high-dimensional regression, foundation models, predictive modeling, and statistical optimization, with applications ranging from healthcare analytics to complex real-world datasets. I aim to bridge mathematical theory with practical machine learning, focusing on model generalization, computational efficiency, and extracting meaningful insights from data.
